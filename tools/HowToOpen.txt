CCTV — how to install
=====================

1. Drag CCTV into the Applications folder.
2. Open CCTV from Applications.

The first time, macOS may say it "can't verify" CCTV, because this free build isn't
notarized by Apple. To open it anyway (only needed once):

  • macOS 15 Sequoia or newer: click Done, then open System Settings → Privacy & Security,
    scroll down and click "Open Anyway" next to CCTV, and confirm.
  • macOS 14 Sonoma: right-click (Control-click) CCTV in Applications → Open → Open.

Or, in Terminal:   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/CCTV.app

3. In CCTV, open Settings, enter your NVR's IP address, username and password,
   click Test Connection, then Save. Allow "Local Network" access when asked.

Requires macOS 14 or newer and an XMEye-based NVR/DVR on your network.
Source code, help and license: see the project page on GitHub.
